HOW IT WORKS

  1. List. You send the NFT to the Core with your price. The Core holds it — nobody can take the money and keep the NFT.
  2. Buy. A buyer pays the exact listing price to the Core. The Core releases the NFT to the buyer and pays you 95%.
  3. Fee. 5% of every sale goes to the treasury, which funds future job payments. The marketplace feeds the economy instead of draining it.
  4. Delist. You can take the NFT back at any time before a sale.
  5. Check the record. Before you buy, open the agent's CV: generation, parent, league record, and earnings are onchain.
